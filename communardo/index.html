Governance

Two roles under the EU AI Act. One fast service.

Your advert names Communardo as both deployer and provider. This is how I would structure the governance work so that it speeds teams up instead of slowing them down.

Deployer

AI Usage Guideline

Clear rules for staff using AI internally: which tools are approved, which data may go where, who to ask. The consultation hour format answers what the guideline cannot.

Provider

Transparency Charter

What clients may expect when Communardo builds AI into their solutions: documentation, transparency obligations, human oversight. Agreed with delivery before it is promised to clients.

Service

Fast lane for approved tools

A short, documented route from "I want to try this tool" to a decision, so the sanctioned path is the quickest one and shadow AI has no reason to exist.

These are proposals for discussion, built on what your advert describes. I would shape them together with the Tech and AI Board.

Dear Communardo recruiting team,

You want the sanctioned path to be the fastest path for AI at Communardo. That is the work I have been doing since 2022 at the Digitalagentur Sachsen, the digital agency of the Free State of Saxony. I find use cases where AI takes work off people's desks, build the prototype and bring the result into the daily work of the authorities that use it.

I am a water management engineer (M.Sc., TU Dresden) and have been working since 2014. Before my current role I spent six years turning flood, low-water and satellite data into concepts and tools for the Saxon environmental administration, most recently as Saxony's river basin coordinator at the environment ministry.

  • Adoption, end to end. I run the AI consultation hour for staff, from everyday questions to the EU AI Act and data protection, and I train state authorities. A station-based course derives individual learning paths for working with language models from survey answers.
  • A use case library with a method behind it. My Use-Case-Radar lets authorities rate AI ideas by benefit and feasibility. A Feasibility-Analyzer checks feasibility.
  • Hands-on and vendor-neutral. I select language models and integrate the results into existing workflows. My tools run on different models. I work with RAG, MCP, n8n, vector databases and Claude Code.
  • Leading without formal authority. As river basin coordinator I prepared national and international committee meetings across authorities and represented Saxony in several of them.
  • Work that others carry on. I co-host PROMPTgenau, the AI podcast of the Digitalagentur Sachsen. My WaterMaskAnalyzer, published as first author in Remote Sensing in 2022, was developed further by the Saxon State Office for Environment, Agriculture and Geology in the Copernicus project BIGFE.

In my first weeks I would meet the process owners in delivery and administration and the volunteers who already carry the AI CoE, take stock of the approved tools and the use case library, and choose one core process to rebuild around AI together with its owner. I would measure that pilot from the first day, so that the Tech and AI Board sees what actually changed.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how I would build the AI CoE in Dresden.
